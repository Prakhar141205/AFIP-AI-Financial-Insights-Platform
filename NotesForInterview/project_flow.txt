Question1. Why convert raw_pdfs to markdown format 
Reason: We convert raw PDFs into Markdown to preserve their original structure, such as headings, paragraphs, lists, and tables, making the content easier for AI models to read, understand, and process.

Why semantic chunking: 
Ans: Step 3: Semantic Chunking

Instead of splitting documents into fixed-size chunks based on character count or token limits, we divide them into smaller chunks based on their semantic meaning and contextual relationships. This ensures that related information stays together, preserving the context and improving retrieval accuracy in the RAG pipeline.
